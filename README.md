# Linky-s-KeeperFX
KeeperFX with one new room and one new creature.

# New Room - The Arena
The Arena allows you to go beyond lvl 10 to a maximum of lvl 12 (5% bonus stats from lvl 10, to make it not OP).
The arena allows you to place 2 creatures in the pit, fighting each other. The highest level creature will win the fight and instantly gain one level, with the ability to go beyond 10.
The fight resulsts in the weaker creature's death.

# New Creature - The Zombie
Attracted by graveyards, like vampires (33% chance to spawn zombie, 66% for vampire), zombies are not particularily strong. They are pretty weak, they lack both damage and health.
But once defeated, their corpses don't rot like other creatures, zombies will come back to life (that's what zombies do, right?).
