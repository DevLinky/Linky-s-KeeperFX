# Linky's KeeperFX
KeeperFX with one new room and two new creatures.

# New Room - The Arena
The Arena allows you to go beyond lvl 10 to a maximum of lvl 12 (5% bonus stats from lvl 10, to make it not OP).
Place 2 creatures in the pit and they will fight each other. The highest level creature will win the fight and gain one level, going beyond 10.
The fight results in the weaker creature's death.

# New Creatures - The Zombie & The Salamender
- The Zombie
  
Attracted to graveyards (33% chance to spawn zombie, 66% vampire), zombies are not particularily strong. They are weak, lacking both damage and health.
But once defeated, their corpses do not rot like other creatures, zombies will come back to life (that's what zombies do, right?).

- The Salamender
  
Salamanders are immuned to lava. Additionnaly, they gain experience if their lair is next to lava.
